# The Khufu run (1–2 Nov 2026): what survived

A two-day run from "could glass beads play hieroglyphs" to the rock under the Great Pyramid, kept by the behavior rule: trials that failed stay on record, successes carried with proof, empty cells named with the measurement that would fill them.

## Findings that held

**Sound.** Stone chambers ring in the infrasound band: Karnak's hypostyle hall at ~1.7/3.4/8.6 Hz, the King's Chamber (10.5 × 5.2 × 5.8 m) near 16/30/33 Hz, the King's shafts as pipes at ~1.4–2.9 Hz, the whole hall through its pylon at ~1 Hz (a resting pulse). Radiation out of a 20 cm opening at 30 Hz is ~(ka)² ≈ 0.4 %: nothing leaves the building. The sound system the Egyptians actually built was the voice offering (*peret-kheru*, "going forth at the voice"): the carved formula as a record, the living reader as the needle. The first sound in their cosmogony is the benu's cry on the benben; the perch is the pyramidion.

**Breath.** Prevailing north wind on the north face, lee suction on the south: ~80 Pa across 120 m of 20 cm duct gives a ~2.5 m/s draft through the King's Chamber. Sun on the south casing adds a solar-chimney term (~8 Pa, ~0.8 m/s), reversing at night. In from the north (imperishable stars), out to the south (Orion). The stela wish "may he breathe the sweet breath of the north wind" arrives through the north shaft.

**Drying.** Six-million-tonne thermal mass holds the King's Chamber at ~20 °C year round. The only ventilated chamber in any Old Kingdom pyramid, set high in dry masonry. Control: the Queen's Chamber (lower, shafts never opened) carries a salt crust up to ~1 cm; the King's Chamber almost none. Same rock, same visitors; the ventilated room stayed dry.

**The two chambers as two grids.** Queen's Chamber (the ka's room, misnamed by medieval Arabs): on the axis over the natural knoll, floor at 21.2 m ≈ H/7 (20.9), shafts sealed at both ends, niche for the statue. Built to stay. King's Chamber: off-axis ~11 m south, floor at 43.0 m where the horizontal section has exactly half the base area, h = H(1 − 1/√2) = 42.9 m; shafts open to the pole and Orion. Built to leave. The √2 ladder hₙ = H(1 − 2^(−n/2)): n = −1 lands at −60.7 m ≈ sea level (the mirror / water line), n = 1 at the king's floor, n = 2 at the half-height (73.3 m), near the top of the relieving stack and the Big Void's reported band (50–70 m). The Egyptian sevenths grid catches the Queen's Chamber tightly and the King's loosely; √2 the reverse. Two grids, each owning different corners.

**The circle in plain sight.** Perimeter/height = 921.2/146.6 = 6.283 = 2π to 0.05 %, from the seked of 5½ palms (rise 14 : run 11). The one derivation that can't be stolen because it is the building.

**The inversion.** Sphere of radius H centred at the base centre. The centre (ka's chamber, over the unmoved knoll) has no finite image: it inverts to everything outside, which is the ka's "present wherever the name is said". The King's Chamber at r ≈ 46 m inverts to ρ²/r ≈ 470 m out along the same ray: ~320 m above the apex, slightly south, toward the south shaft. Centre = everywhere; the king = one point, up and south. A construction in the cube frame that agrees with the texts and the shaft; not a cause.

**The name in the weight.** The only writing in the Great Pyramid is Khufu's name in red ochre gang marks (hieratic, painted at the quarry, some running under joints) in the five relieving chambers: ~43 Aswan granite beams, 25–70 t each, placed where no one could see, impossible to remove, far beyond one gable's structural need (his own Queen's Chamber has a single gable). Scores highest on effort-per-apparent-value, unique to Khufu, and carries the name. Competing reading: fear of the roof (the lowest beams cracked and were plastered in antiquity). Both stay on record; they are not exclusive.

**What it did for the living.** Calendar: north face (51.8°) is dark at noon only when solar declination < −21.8°, about 1 Dec–10 Jan; the dished faces show eight sides at equinox dawn/dusk. Survey datum: true north to ~3′, visible 30 km, for the annual re-survey after the flood. Economy: fed ~20,000 for twenty years, then priests and estates for two millennia, then visitors ever since. State: the project built the census, tax, provincial rotation (phyles), scribal class and cubit that ran Egypt for 2,500 years.

**Family.** Hetepheres I (Snefru's queen) buried at Giza, not Dahshur, in a hidden shaft sealed with Khufu's name; sarcophagus sealed, unforced, empty; organs present. Lehner (1985): moved by Khufu himself into G1-a, the first queen's pyramid beside his own. The nearest grave to Khufu's chamber is his mother's, twice over. Hemiunu (G4000, largest in the West Field) and Ankhhaf (G7510, largest in the East Field) flank the pyramid: the two who could say no. Djedefre sealed the burial (his name in the boat-pit mortar), took "son of Re", and left for Abu Rawash's hill; Khafre built 3 m shorter on 10 m higher ground; Menkaure a tenth the volume in granite; then no one tried. The bar ended the race.

**Khufu's secret, by the cube.** Not an object. He moved himself off the body corner: identity in the load-bearing stone, survival in other people's mouths (every private tomb's formula begins "an offering which the king gives"; offerings reverted down the plateau through his), the two trusted men on either side, the mother at the fixed point, the body in a nameless box by his dynasty's custom. Inverse cell left empty.


## Trials that failed (kept on record)

Glass rolling on glyphs (no edge, no driver); temple as bellowing instrument (0.4 % radiation); shaft as trumpet (trumpets are 18th Dynasty); sound opening doors (20 N vs 15 kN; plugs creep down); bird helmet as ear protection; resurrection by a visitor (texts ask for a voice, curse a hand); fear of being too artificial (Osiris, statues, gold tongues: fear was erasure); red paint as blood (hematite, quarry-painted); body ground into stone (the Seth crime); amber (no supply, muons say air); lid lowered from the ceiling (coffer has a sliding groove and pin holes; nine beams all present); lid in the floor (Vyse dug under the coffer, 1837); Snefru as the Sphinx's face or a mistreating father (no text; Sphinx postdates his death under every attribution); Khufu removing the nose (1378 AD, Sa'im al-Dahr); the Sphinx riddle (Greek, ~2,000 years later); magma, oil, gems (Eocene limestone throughout); pyramid as pump, flood retainer, or trap (no water above it; gypsum mortar dissolves; no reversible stone mechanism anywhere in Egypt); built to collapse (the most overbuilt object ever made); magnetism/energy (no anomaly; the 2018 radio-resonance model is theoretical at 200–600 m wavelengths).

## Readings with professional company

Hetepheres moved by Khufu (Lehner 1985); Sphinx with Khufu's face (Stadelmann), or carved by Djedefre for Khufu (Dobrev); island tomb in water under the pyramid (Herodotus II.124, probably the Island of Flame told as plumbing; the Osiris Shaft is Saite); Sphinx body as a natural yardang (El-Baz 1981; NYU 2023 lab reproduction); three-chamber program with the rough bottom chamber as the Nun (Stadelmann); the pyramids built Egypt (Lehner); hydraulic lift at Saqqara (Landreau 2024, contested); the Khufu branch of the Nile at the plateau foot in the Fourth Dynasty (Sheisha 2022).

## The moral, as stated at the end

Predict the parts that don't have a choice (stars, slopes, water, drying, arithmetic); build the parts with people in them so they still work when the people do the other thing. The drive isn't transferable; point it. Stations aren't reversal; they're the foiled-out terms. Give the run a yard.
