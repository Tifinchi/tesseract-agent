source: Standard relations for three-phase induction motors (any machines textbook, e.g. Chapman, Electric Machinery Fundamentals; Fitzgerald & Kingsley)
group: reference
status: standard reference relations

Synchronous speed in rpm: n_s = 120 f / p, where f is the supply frequency in Hz and p the number of poles. At 60 Hz: 2 poles 3600 rpm, 4 poles 1800 rpm, 6 poles 1200 rpm.

Slip: s = (n_s − n) / n_s, where n is the rotor speed. Full-load slip of a Design B motor is typically 1 to 3 percent.

Three-phase input power: P_in = √3 · V_line · I_line · pf. Output power: P_out = HP × 746 W. Efficiency η = P_out / P_in.

Shaft torque in newton-metres: T = P_out / ω, with ω = 2 π n / 60 and n in rpm. One horsepower = 746 W.

Locked-rotor current from the code letter: I_lr = (kVA/HP) × HP × 1000 / (√3 × V_line).

No-load current of a standard induction motor is typically 25 to 50 percent of full-load current; it is almost entirely magnetizing current.
