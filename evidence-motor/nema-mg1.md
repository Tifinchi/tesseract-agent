Table 12-12, full-load efficiencies of NEMA Premium (energy-efficient, 60 Hz) random-wound motors, open and enclosed, 4-pole: 7.5 HP 91.7 % (open) and 91.7 % (enclosed); 10 HP 91.7 % (open) and 91.7 % (enclosed); 15 HP 93.0 % (open) and 92.4 % (enclosed). Nominal efficiency is the average of a population; the minimum (guaranteed) efficiency is one band lower on the NEMA efficiency scale (for 91.7 % nominal, 90.2 % minimum).

Table 10-5, locked-rotor kVA per horsepower by code letter: F 5.0 to 5.59; G 5.6 to 6.29; H 6.3 to 7.09; J 7.1 to 7.99; K 8.0 to 8.99.

Design B: locked-rotor torque not less than about 150 to 175 percent of full-load torque for motors in this size range; breakdown (pull-out) torque not less than 200 percent of full-load torque; full-load slip less than 5 percent.
