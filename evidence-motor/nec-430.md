Table 430.250 (full-load current, three-phase AC motors, induction type, for sizing conductors and overcurrent devices): 10 HP at 460 V: 14 A. 7½ HP at 460 V: 11 A. 15 HP at 460 V: 21 A. The table values are used instead of the nameplate current for conductor and overcurrent sizing (430.6), and are generally somewhat higher than typical nameplate values.

430.22: conductors supplying a single continuous-duty motor shall have an ampacity not less than 125 percent of the motor full-load current rating as determined by 430.6(A)(1). For 14 A that is 17.5 A.

430.52 and Table 430.52: for a Design B squirrel-cage motor the maximum rating of an inverse-time circuit breaker is 250 percent of the full-load current; for 14 A that is 35 A. The maximum for a nontime-delay fuse is 300 percent (42 A).

Table 310.16, copper, 75 °C column: 14 AWG 20 A, 12 AWG 25 A, 10 AWG 35 A, 8 AWG 50 A.
