## Given

Motor M1 is a 10 HP, 460 V, 60 Hz, three-phase NEMA Design B premium-efficiency squirrel-cage induction motor; its plate reads full-load current 12.4 A, full-load speed 1775 rpm, code letter G, nominal efficiency 91.7 %. The 2026 National Electrical Code is the edition on file.

## Three questions on the motor

For sizing the conductors and the branch-circuit breaker, the current to use is the code table value, 14 A, not the plate's 12.4 A. The conductors must therefore have an ampacity of at least 17.5 A, and an inverse-time breaker may be rated up to 35 A; if 35 A will not carry the starting current, the breaker may be increased to no more than 56 A.

The locked-rotor current implied by code letter G is between 70 A and 79 A, which is about 6.3 times the plate's full-load current and sits under the code's maximum locked-rotor current of 81 A for a 10 hp, 460 V Design B motor.

The full-load efficiency is 91.7 %, reached by three independent routes: the plate, the NEMA Premium table, and the solved equivalent circuit. The no-load current, 5.40 A, is about 44 % of the plate's full-load current.

## Two statements that live only in dated pages

In January 2016 the current edition of the National Electrical Code was the 2014 edition. By June 2021 the current edition was the 2020 edition.
