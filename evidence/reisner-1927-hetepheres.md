Reisner reports the discovery in 1925 of a 30 m shaft (G7000x) east of the Great Pyramid containing the gilded furniture, inlaid boxes, canopic chest with preserved organs in natron solution, and the alabaster sarcophagus of Queen Hetepheres I, wife of Snefru and mother of Khufu. The sarcophagus was sealed and intact but, when opened, empty. Seal impressions in the deposit carry Khufu's name. Reisner proposed that her original tomb at Dahshur had been robbed and the body lost, and that officials reburied the surviving equipment at Giza without informing the king.
