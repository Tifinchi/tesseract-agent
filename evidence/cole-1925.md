Cole's survey of the base from the recovered corner sockets and casing lines gives the side lengths: north 230.253 m, south 230.454 m, east 230.391 m, west 230.357 m; mean 230.364 m. The perimeter is therefore 921.455 m. The sides deviate from true north by about 3 arcminutes (north side 2' 28" west of north; the mean of the four sides about 3' 6"). Cole did not determine the height.
