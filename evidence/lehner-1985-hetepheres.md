Lehner argues that the shaft tomb G7000x east of the Great Pyramid, found by Reisner in 1925 with Hetepheres I's furniture, canopic chest and sealed but empty sarcophagus, was her original burial made under Khufu, and that Khufu later moved her body into the first of the queens' pyramids beside his own (G1-a) when it was built, leaving the shaft burial sealed. On this reading the empty sarcophagus was not the result of a robbery concealed from the king but of a reburial ordered by him.
