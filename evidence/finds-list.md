Great Pyramid of Khufu: granite sarcophagus in the King's Chamber, lid missing; chamber walls undecorated; in the Queen's Chamber air shafts, a diorite ball, a copper hook and a cedar plank (the Dixon relics, 1872); hieroglyphic graffiti including Khufu's name in the relieving chambers; no human remains recorded.

Khafre's pyramid: granite sarcophagus with lid; bull bones found inside, a later intrusion; no human remains. Menkaure's pyramid: basalt sarcophagus with palace-façade decoration (lost at sea in 1838); a later wooden anthropoid coffin inscribed with Menkaure's name; bones of a woman from a later burial.
