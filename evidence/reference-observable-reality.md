group: reference
status: bedrock; add a line only for something anyone can observe, never for a result

Unsupported objects denser than air fall; stone, metal, wood and people do not float in air. Water flows downhill and finds its own level; it does not rise in an open channel without being pushed or pumped. Nothing lifts itself: raising a mass takes work from outside it. Heat flows from hot to cold; a cool room does not warm a hot one. Sound needs air or a solid to travel and gets quieter with distance; a sound that cannot be heard outside a wall was not heard outside the wall. Fire needs fuel, heat and air. Limestone and gypsum dissolve slowly in water; dry stone stays dry when kept from water. A sealed container does not lose its contents. The sun rises in the east and sets in the west; shadows point away from the sun and are longest near sunrise and sunset. A human cannot lift a tonne, breathe under water, or live without water for weeks. Light does not bend around corners in open air. A thing cannot be in two places at once. A machine with no moving parts does no mechanical work. A building that has stood for thousands of years was not built to collapse. Nothing on Earth, left to itself, moves faster than it was thrown, dropped, or driven.
