Speed of sound in dry air at 20 °C: 343 m/s. Density of air at sea level, 20 °C: 1.204 kg/m³. One inch = 0.0254 m; one foot = 0.3048 m. Standard gravity g = 9.80665 m/s².

Room acoustics: the lowest axial resonance of a closed rectangular room along a dimension L is f = c / (2 L). A pipe closed at one end resonates at f = c / (4 L); open at both ends at f = c / (2 L). Dynamic pressure of a wind of speed U is ½ ρ U². Stack (chimney) pressure for a column of height Δh warmed by ΔT above ambient T is ρ g Δh ΔT / T.

The circumference of a circle of radius r is 2 π r. A seked of 5½ palms per cubit of 7 palms corresponds to a slope of 7/5.5 = 14/11 (rise over run); for a square pyramid of base b and height h with that slope, h = (14/11) · (b/2) and the perimeter 4b divided by the height is 4b / ((14/11)(b/2)) = 88/14 = 6.2857, which is 2π to 0.04 %.
